Orbit fonts subset from hub/web/fonts/DMSans.ttf and BarlowCondensed-Bold.ttf.
DM Sans uses the original weight 400 instance, ASCII and general punctuation.
Barlow Condensed Bold uses Latin-1 and general punctuation. Other characters use sans-serif fallback.
Subset with fontTools pyftsubset and WOFF2 compression. Original OFL licenses included.
