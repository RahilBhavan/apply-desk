# Privacy

Apply Desk runs on your Mac. The local desk listens on loopback and requires its per-process request token for data access. It does not provide a shared club account or a hosted resume database.

Your profile, PDF snapshots, extracted resume text, job URLs, captured questions, answers, draft proposals, browser observations and member-reported submissions stay in your local workspace. The Chrome helper communicates with the installed local native host. Filling an employer form can trigger that site's normal uploads or autosave; review the employer's privacy policy before using its application.

Connecting T3 allows the application context you request through its tools to reach your selected model provider. This can include profile facts and extracted resume text. Your provider's terms and retention settings apply. Apply Desk does not borrow the maintainer's account, ship provider keys, make hidden model calls or start recurring model jobs.

Shared job CSV exports contain company, role title and reviewed public opportunity URLs only. Personalized/unrecognized links are excluded. Do not share a private workspace folder, browser profile, PDF export or native-host configuration as a club list. URL structure cannot independently prove a page is public; members review outgoing links before download.

There is no product telemetry, advertising or sale of member data. The local connector and browser helper record their latest successful contact locally so setup can display a connection status. Filesystem permissions are not encryption.

To remove local data, quit Apply Desk and delete `~/Library/Application Support/Apply Desk`. This removes profiles, resumes, answers and local history. Removing the app alone preserves data for updates. Other copies or backups you made remain your responsibility.
